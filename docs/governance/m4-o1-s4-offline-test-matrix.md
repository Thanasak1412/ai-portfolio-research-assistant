## Shared fixture and evidence rules

Every ID below is **Phase A design acceptance → Phase B authorized offline execution** and **DESIGN_ONLY / NOT_EXECUTED**. Variants are independently observed within the named case. Expected results are proposed safety oracles, not approved storage, event precedence, response codes or retry rules.

- Start each variant with synthetic incident `INC-SYN-A`, notification intent `INT-SYN-A`, immutable payload `PAYLOAD-SYN-A`, and fake clock `T0`. When needed, use separate provider message `MSG-SYN-A` and callback `EVT-SYN-A` references. No real account data, keys, endpoints or mailbox addresses. Observe only sanitized intent/outcome/history snapshots, attempt and side-effect counts, and captured output surfaces.
- The future fake provider substitutes for the **Email Transport Adapter only**. Use synthetic provider responses, a separately controlled persistence fault, and a fake clock; no network or live provider quota consumption. Proposed labels `PENDING`, `SUBMITTING`, `SUBMITTED`, `ACCEPTED`, `DELIVERED`, `DELAYED`, `BOUNCED`, `SUPPRESSED`, `FAILED` and `UNKNOWN` describe evidence, not a mandated sequence or database schema.
- An HTTP callback success acknowledges durable email-event receipt under the eventual design; provider acceptance is not recipient-server delivery; recipient-server delivery is not inbox placement, Gmail forwarding or human acknowledgement. Only an actual operator action with actor and UTC time establishes human acknowledgement.
- No case may claim, publish, retry, dead-letter or acknowledge a **Transaction outbox** event, use a fake receiver/no-op publisher, or activate a worker/receiver. Under [ADR-023](../adr/ADR-023-transaction-publication-activation-prerequisite.md) receiver absence continues to mean zero such activity; [ADR-022](../adr/ADR-022-transaction-outbox-delivery-operational-policy.md) settings do not supply email retry defaults, and [ADR-024](../adr/ADR-024-m4-holdings-durable-handoff.md) architecture approval does not authorize activation.
- Source shorthand in each row: **R** = Resend proposal section; **P** = review packet section or clause; **O** = formal O1 record subsection. **TBD** in every decision cell means **TBD — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED** for the named clause.
